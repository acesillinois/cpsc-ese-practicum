## SIGNIFICANCE OF PLANT CULTIVAR AS A PREDICTOR OF INSECT-CROP INTERACTIONS
### Overview

<p>
  Many crops dependent on pollination for proliferation are not reaching optimal yield capacity. The
cultivar of the plant has been shown to influence the frequency of pollinator insect visitation, though
referenced data is limited in both the plant and insect species studied — highbush blueberry and bees,
respectively. A better understanding of which cultivars are preferred by various pollinator insects can
provide farmers with information for strategic crop selection
</p>

### Goals and Objectives

<p>
  This project aims to further establish the causal effect between plant cultivar and pollination frequency.
Through regression modeling and post-fit analysis, equations for more accurate predictions on future
crop yield may be developed.
</p>
