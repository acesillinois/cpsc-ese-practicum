## Investigation of Information Concent Variability Conditional on Encoding and Tokenization Choices in Neural Networks

This project aims to improve plant gene expression models by leveraging deep learning architectures to
address limitations found in previous studies. 
<p>
  Existing approaches, which rely on traditional machine learning techniques such as Random Forest, Support Vector Regression, and Gradient Boosting, often
suffer from reduced predictive accuracy. To overcome these constraints, a transformer architecture will be
implemented to capture long-range regulatory mechanisms, specifically modeled around the complex
interactions between distal chromatin modifications and proximal promoter regions. 
</p>
<p>
  Additionally, incorporating environmental and climate context provides predictive power, given that plant epigenetic
mechanisms are connected to environmental adaptation. By integrating these variables, the model will
account for gene by environment (G x E) interactions and forecast expression variations across diverse
conditions. 
  </p>
  <p>
I plan on using a multimodal deep learning framework where one stream processes epigenetic
data via the transformer model, while a parallel multilayer perceptron (MLP) handles geospatial and
climate inputs. 
    </p>
    <p>
  These features will then be unified through a Cross-Attention fusion layer to derive the final
gene expression estimates. Ultimately, performance will be benchmarked against the prior models to
quantify the gains achieved by these advanced methodology improvements.
</p>
