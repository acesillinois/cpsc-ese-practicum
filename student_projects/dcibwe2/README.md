# INCLUDING TRYPSIN INHIBITOR VARIABLE IN IMPROVE ACCURACY AMINO ACID
DIGESTIBILITY PREDICTIVE MODELS FOR SOYBEAN MEAL

<p>Soybean meal (SBM) remains the most utilized protein feedstuff in poultry production
because of its balanced amino acid profile. Currently, diets are formulated based on
amino acid (AA) digestibility values rather than total crude protein (CP) or AA content of
the feedstuff. There are antinutritional factors (ANFs) that reduce AA digestibility, lowering
protein quality of the SBM. Trypsin inhibitor (TI) is an ANF of economic significance in
poultry feeding. Apart from reducing AA digestibility, TI also reduces feed intake and
consequently growth.</p>
<p>
To meet AA requirements of chickens, predictive models (digestibility coefficients) are
used during feed formulation, to determine the AA digestibility values in SBM. Most of the
AA digestibility predictive models assume that SBM was properly processed, such that
the TI has been reduced to a minimal level within the safe baseline. However, with varying
processing methods, TI levels vary in SBM samples of the same genotype and produced
with similar environmental conditions. Therefore, including the variable of TI content in
the AA digestibility models can help to improve the accuracy of the predictive models,
allowing necessary adjustments in feed formulas.
  </p>
  <p>
To achieve this a study will be conducted using available data from previous feeding
experiments that focused on AA digestibility in SBM and analyzed TI of the SBM.
Calculations including the following will be conducted
    <list>
A. Observed digestibility – predicted digestibility without including TI.
B. Observed digestibility – predicted digestibility with TI included.
C. For each AA, the answer in B will be subtracted from A, to measure the
      </list>
difference between the models. The models using TI will be modified to improve
their accuracy
</p>
